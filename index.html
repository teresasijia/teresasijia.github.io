I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics at Texas A&M University, on the job market in 2026–27.

I work in experimental and behavioural economics. My research studies how people make decisions when they do not know the odds, and how well the standard models of that behaviour predict choices they were not fitted to.

My job market paper asks whether a measured ambiguity attitude transfers across settings: if we learn how someone treats a vaguely described bet, can we predict how they treat a sharper one? More on the research page.
