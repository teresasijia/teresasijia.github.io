Job market paper

Does Ambiguity Attitude Travel? Out-of-Sample Prediction Across Ambiguity LevelsDraft upcoming
with Alexander L. Brown

Models of choice under ambiguity are usually judged by how well they fit the choices used to estimate them. This paper judges them by prediction instead. Subjects face bets on an urn of unknown composition; an adaptive procedure estimates each subject's parameters from a short sequence of questions, and the fitted models are then scored on held-out questions they never saw.

Draft coming soon. In the meantime you can run a short version of the elicitation and see what each model infers about you.
